Note:
The defense follows five sections that mirror the five-layer architecture of the solution. First, the engineering context: why FWA migration is a constrained multi-criteria problem, not a simple commercial targeting exercise. Second, the data foundation: how heterogeneous telecom sources — CRM, usage records, RAN KPIs, address-level coverage, and service consumption — were cleaned and consolidated into a single subscriber-month Analytical Base Table. Third, eligibility and coverage qualification: the rule-based scoring engine that filters the base according to radio feasibility, fibre cannibalisation, and network capacity. Note that the third section is where the central finding of this project emerges: the FWA opportunity is bounded by coverage, not by capacity. Fourth, decision intelligence: how the eligible population is segmented into personas, prioritised via multi-criteria analysis, and scored for churn risk. Fifth, campaign execution: the two-tier offer recommendation, the PRB capacity validation, and the final campaign action list prepared for pilot review.
