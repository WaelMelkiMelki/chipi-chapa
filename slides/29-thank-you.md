Thank you for your attention. This project has been carried out with Huawei Northern Africa, under the joint supervision of Mr. Tabbane Sami at SUP'COM, Mr. Abdelkefi Slim and Mr. Bensalem Mohamed at Huawei. I would like to thank them for their guidance, their availability, and their technical insight throughout this work. The central finding of this project is a single sentence: the FWA migration opportunity is coverage-constrained, not capacity-constrained. The addressable market is bounded by the physical radio environment, and the path to expanding it is network investment, not capacity provisioning. What the pipeline produces — the analytical base table, the eligibility engine, the segmentation, the prioritisation, the churn model, and the recommendation and capacity validation layers — is a decision-support framework that is now ready for pilot execution. I am happy to answer your questions.
