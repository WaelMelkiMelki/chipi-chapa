Note:
The project was carried out with Huawei Northern Africa, based in Tunis. Huawei was founded in 1987 and today operates in more than 170 countries, serving over three billion people. Its activities span telecom networks, broadband connectivity, cloud, intelligent connectivity, and smart devices — all directly relevant to this project, which sits at the intersection of radio access network planning and customer analytics. Northern Africa is a region where the operator challenges — broadband expansion, mobile data growth, radio resource optimisation — match the FWA migration problem precisely. The project work was guided by Huawei's three strategic principles: customer orientation, focus on hardworking individuals, and a long-term commitment to telecom infrastructure. These principles shaped the engineering approach: design for real operator outcomes, validate every decision step, and never lose sight of the subscriber behind each data point.
