Note:
Welcome. This final-year project was carried out with Huawei Northern Africa and supervised jointly by SUP'COM and Huawei. The topic sits at the intersection of 5G radio planning, customer analytics, and commercial strategy: how to identify which existing mobile subscribers can be migrated to 5G Fixed Wireless Access, which offer each candidate should receive, and whether the radio access network can safely absorb the migration — without cannibalising fibre or overloading the RAN. Over the next 20 minutes I will walk through the engineering problem, the data pipeline, the decision-intelligence layers, and the resulting campaign action list.
